Honours Thesis - The Distribution of Star Formation in Merging Galaxies

Used spatially resolved spectroscopy from the SAMI Galaxy Survey, combined with GAMA morphology and merger classifications, to map how galaxy mergers reshape star formation. Mergers boost star formation everywhere, but the strongest bursts sit in the nuclear core, with the size of the effect shifting by merger ratio, pair separation, and morphology.

Colour Theory 101 - Mixing Galaxies

Summary project using MUSE VLT IFU datacubes to classify galaxies into pair, merging, and post-coalescence stages. Sliced emission-line data (Hα, [OIII]) to trace star-forming regions and built g–r colour density plots showing pairs skew blue and post-coalescence systems skew red, with redshift estimates matching NED values to within 0.002.
