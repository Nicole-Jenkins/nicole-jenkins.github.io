Useful Links This page is a running list of things I find useful, such as research links, what imaging software or art supplies I use, and creative or have helpful websites. This space will also host a few behind-the-scenes tutorials where I unpack the reality of processing deep space data and share some of my favorite sketching techniques.

Astro & Research Links

UniSQ Astrophysics - the research group homepage.

QUASAR Collaboration - homepage of the QUASAR astro collaboration between UniSQ, UQ, and QUT.

Imaging Software & Tools

PixInsight - astrophotography processing software, and my main creative processing workflow.

RC-Astro - home of the BlurXTerminator, NoiseXTerminator, and StarXTerminator plugins I use in PixInsight.

TelescopeLive - an online database of raw astroimages procured by telescopes around the world, available for astrophotography image editing when you don't have your own telescope.

Art Supplies

Canson Mi Tientes Paper - my preferred art paper for most of my work.

Prismacolor Premier Pencils - one of two brands I use in almost every artwork.

Faber-Castell Polychromos Pencils - the other brand of pencils I use in almost all of my art.
